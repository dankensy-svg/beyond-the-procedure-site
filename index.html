The show

About the show

Everything that happens around a procedure.

The drive. The hospital. The physician who travels. The community that keeps its care. Beyond the Procedure talks with the people building a new model for interventional radiology in rural America.

Every episode starts with people and plain-language context. No background in medicine or hospital operations needed.

01

Rural America

The patient and the long drive, the patient who never makes it, and what local care changes for a community.

02

What administrators need to hear

Straight answers for hospital leaders: what a program asks of their teams and what to realistically expect.

03

A partnership, not coverage

How Travelier works with each hospital toward its own goals, beyond short-term coverage or a single hire.
